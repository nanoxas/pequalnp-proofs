QUOTA MATCHING NEEDS GENERAL UNUSED-GATE CREDITS

Status: reproducible finite audits. No Lean proof or SAT solver is claimed.
All examples satisfy the hypotheses and conclusion of L-meta-997. They
refute auxiliary resource-selection statements that are stronger than997.

1. NOTATION AND THE EXACT GAP

Let Y have size k, h=AND(Y), and z=NOT h. For a gate w, U(w) is its
singleton-positive support, equivalently its singleton support after z is
replaced by true. V(w) is its positive support on Y union{p}, p outside Y,
equivalently that shifted support after z is replaced by false.

For target S, the C1 private pool G_S consists of gates w other than z
with |U(w)|>=2 and U(w) subset S. For heavy T disjoint Y, the C0 pool H_T
consists of OR gates false on the complement of T and with |V(w)|>=2.
Every H_T gate is C0-sound for T, and V(w) subset T.

The relevant quotas are min(k,|S|)-1 for light S and |T|-1 for heavy T.
A refund requires U(w) to equal an entire target and V(w) to equal an
entire heavy target. Containment and partial overlaps do not suffice.

2. FULL-HEAVY-OR QUOTAS: TWO SMALL AUXILIARY CLAIMS FAIL

audit.py generates two families with pools consisting entirely of OR
gates and excluding every direct NOT reader. Each light target uses the
construction

  S={y_i} union P,
  w_j=(p_j AND z) OR(t_j AND h),
  M=OR_j w_j,
  light_output=y_i OR M.

The p_j cycle through P, and T={t_j} is a fresh heavy target. Put
Q=AND_{y in Y}(y OR OR(T))=h OR OR(T). The heavy output is

  (Q AND z) OR(M AND h).

If h=false, M=OR(P), Q=OR(T), and z=true. If h=true, every y_i is true,
M=OR(T), Q=true, and z=false. These identities prove both outputs exactly.

For k=5, |P|=4, |T|=8, the minimum overlap between a light quota4 and a
heavy OR quota7 is2, while R=1. All131,072 rows of this17-input circuit
are checked. It refutes minimum-full-quota overlap<=R.

An affine-plane order5 construction shares the narrow p AND z gates
among30 light targets. Here k=6, each light target has size6, and each
fresh heavy target has size9. The exact minimum full-quota overlap is90,
R=30, and there are55 direct NOT readers. Hence90>30+55. The claimed
reader-budget bound fails even after all readers are removed from the
candidate OR pools. Its301-input original-output correctness follows
from the displayed generator identities, independently checked on every
abstract Boolean case. All662 singleton, shifted and soundness rows are
also evaluated. A full301-variable truth table is not claimed.

The full-quota examples leave abundant C1-private or unused-gate credit.
They do not refute residual-deficit accounting after those credits are
used. The next example addresses that distinction directly.

3. RESIDUAL G/H POOL MATCHING PLUS REFUNDS ALSO FAILS

residual_audit.py builds a19-input circuit. Points0..6 are the nonzero
vectors1..7 of F_2^3. Set Y={0,1,2}, one Fano line. The other six Fano
lines are light targets S_1,...,S_6; each has one point y_i in Y and two
points P_i outside Y. Their pairwise intersections have size1.

Take one heavy target T={7,...,18}, disjoint from the Fano plane. Partition
it into six pairs T_i. For each light target use the two-point version of
the switch above:

  M_i=OR_{j=1,2}((p_ij AND z) OR(t_ij AND h)),
  light_output_i=y_i OR M_i.

Share each p AND z gate globally among the three light targets containing
p. Define M=OR_i M_i, Q=AND_{y in Y}(y OR OR(T)), and

  heavy_output=(Q AND z) OR(M AND h).

The same two-case identity proves every output exactly. The evaluator
also checks all524,288 Boolean assignments independently, all earlier-read
and fan-in conditions, the sole NOT, its complete low-weight window, all
target intersections, and structural liveness of every gate.

There are89 gates and seven targets. The exact eligible-resource counts
are:

  each of the six light G_i:       2 gates, quota2;
  heavy G_T:                      4 gates, quota11;
  heavy H_T:                     12 gates;
  union of all G_i:               16 gates;
  union of all G_i and H_T:        21 gates;
  total target quota:             23;
  exact refund count:              1.

The heavy C1-private deficit is7. The heavy H_T contributes only5 gates
outside the full private union. Thus two units remain unpaid by all
eligible G/H resources, and one unit remains unpaid after the exact
refund credit is added. This is a Hall obstruction on the entire target
family: no choice, exchange order, or matching inside those pools can
repair it.

The example leaves68 gates outside the eligible union. Its actual997
inequality is30<=89+1+7=97. It therefore isolates a required feature of any
successful charging proof: some general unused gates must be eligible as
credits. A theorem confined to existing target-owned G/H resources plus
refunds is false, even when it first credits every C1 private gate.

4. ALLOWING EVERY SOUND HEAVY GATE STILL LEAVES A GAP

The heavy pool can be enlarged to include AND gates as well as OR gates,
retaining C0 soundness and shifted width at least2. This adds the final
M AND h context gate in Section3 and repairs that example's one-unit gap.

The same family with seven light triples defeats the enlarged pool. Keep
the six Fano triples, add one target {0,7,8}, and use a fresh14-point heavy
target {9,...,22}. The circuit has23 inputs and104 gates. Its private union
has18 gates, the all-operation heavy pool has15 gates, and their union
has25 gates. The quotas total27, while the refund count is1. Thus one
unit remains unpaid. The actual997 inequality is35<=104+1+8=113.

all_ops_audit.py verifies all56 required singleton, shifted, complement,
zero and Y rows, and every input/read/liveness condition. Output correctness
on arbitrary original assignments follows from an independently evaluated
abstract generator identity: three Y bits, seven independent values
s_i=OR(P_i), and seven independent values t_i=OR(T_i). All131,072 assignments
to those17 abstract bits are checked. Every original assignment maps to
one of those abstract assignments. A full23-variable truth-table audit is
not claimed.

The general count for L light triples in this pattern is

  |G_all|=2L+4, |H_all|=2L+1, |G_all intersect H_all|=L+1,
  |G_all union H_all|=3L+4, demand=4L-1, R=1.

The unpaid remainder after refunds is L-6. Both construction and counts
were independently reviewed. These examples have only one deficient heavy
class, so the separate checked OneHeavyDeficit theorem already proves their
actual weighted inequality using the outside-wide gates.

5. A PRECISE REMAINING EXCHANGE FORMULATION

For a target subfamily A, let G_A be its private-gate union and H_A the
heavy-pool union over its heavy members. The pools are disjoint within
each family. They already satisfy

  |G_A union H_A| >= sum_{heavy i in A} quota_i + |G_A minus H_A|.

Thus heavy-only subfamilies cannot obstruct ordinary quota matching.
Any obstruction includes light demand. Every private AND in G_A lies in
G_A minus H_A, since heavy pools contain only OR gates. The checked
private OR/AND dichotomy and deficient-heavy AND bound provide real
credits there. However Section3 shows that these pools alone are still
insufficient in general.

One useful remaining target is a charging map from the unpaid residual
Hall cut into gates outside the selected G/H resources, with exact
refunds providing the additional permitted capacity. Such a map must use
the circuit's two-window causal structure or its h=false negative rows.
No such general map is proved by these examples.

6. REPRODUCTION AND SCOPE

  python3 main-997/quota-audit/audit.py
  python3 main-997/quota-audit/residual_audit.py
  python3 main-997/quota-audit/all_ops_audit.py

The first writes circuits.json and results.json. The second writes
residual-circuit.json and residual-results.json, including the entire
gate list, all eligible pools, exact refunds and truth-vector hashes.
The third writes all-ops-circuit.json and all-ops-results.json. All scripts
use only the Python standard library. Results record the source and
input hashes, and explicitly set lean_checked=false and solver_used=false.

These are finite counterobstructions to auxiliary counting claims. The
unrestricted L-meta-997 statement remains open in this research record.
