CASE13: DATA AUDIT AND EQUALITY-PARTITION EXCLUSION

Status: the numerical/family/baseline data are independently checked on all
8192 inputs. The exclusion below is a paper proof independently reviewed by
the bounds worker. It is not a Lean theorem or a SAT exhaustion result. No
solver was started for this case.

The exact search-compatible input is case.json. The target sets are

  S0={3,4,5,6,7,8,9,10},
  S1={0,3,11}, S2={1,4,11}, S3={2,5,11},
  S4={0,6,12}, S5={1,7,12}, S6={2,8,12},
  S7={0,1,9}, S8={0,2,10}, S9={1,2,3},
  Y={0,1,2}.

All45 target intersections have size at most1. There is one heavy target,
S0, and nine light triples. Thus n=13,m=10,k=3,F=35,H=1. The checked light
private count plus minterm budget gives B+k=20+3=23<=N; the exact posted
refund includes at least the heavy output, so R>=1. A violating circuit must
therefore satisfy N=23 and R=1. The complementary heavy baseline is19 and
would leave four extra gates, but the light partition is exact and stronger.

Let C1 be the monotone circuit obtained by replacing the unique NOT gate z
with constant true. Its outputs agree with the original exact set-ORs on
every input of weight below3. In particular their singleton supports are
exactly the corresponding S_i. For each i define

  Gi={gate wires w other than z: 2<=|U(w)| and U(w) subset S_i},

where U(w) is the singleton support in C1. The Gi are pairwise disjoint by
linearity. The checked light theorem gives |Gi|>=2 for every i. The checked
minterm theorem gives two prefix AND gates with empty singleton support;
they are outside every Gi and outside z. With N=23, these are all the gates:
each Gi has exactly two members, there are exactly two empty-support gates
outside their union, and z has singleton support equal to all13 inputs.
Every target is a proper subset of those13 inputs.

Consider the first gate in Gi. An AND with singleton support of size at
least2 must have every effective operand support of size at least2. A raw
input, invalid reference, or empty-support gate is therefore impossible.
An operand in Gj for j!=i is impossible because its support would contain
the output's at least two points inside S_i intersect S_j. If every operand
were z, the output support would be universal, not contained in proper S_i.
Thus a wide AND must have an earlier Gi operand, contradicting firstness.

The first Gi gate is consequently an OR. A wide child must be in Gi: a
foreign wide support or universal z cannot be contained in its S_i support.
There is no earlier Gi gate, so its two effective operands must be distinct
raw singleton inputs. Its singleton support therefore has size exactly2.

Now consider the second Gi gate. At an AND it has a Gi child, as above, so
its support is contained in the first gate's two-point support. At an OR,
the only possible wide child is the first Gi gate; the other operand adds
at most one raw singleton. If both children are narrow, the support has size
at most2. Thus every gate in Gi has singleton support of size at most3.

The actual output for S0 is neither a raw input, an invalid wire, nor z.
Its C1 singleton support is S0 of size8, so it lies in G0. This contradicts
the preceding bound3. Therefore no23-gate violation exists, and the original
L-meta-997 inequality holds for this fixed family and Y on paper.

The generic version uses a support-cover DAG: choose an earlier same-class
child at each wide AND and retain both children at each OR. All nontrivial
internal supports stay in Gi, and raw singleton stops cover S_i. A binary
union network then requires at least |S_i|-1 Gi OR gates. This proves that
equality N=B+k is impossible whenever all targets are proper and one target
has size greater than k. An oversized heavy target automatically forces all
targets proper under the original linearity hypothesis.

Baseline audit: baseline.json has25 OR gates computing all ten outputs,
plus two ANDs and the NOT computing NOT(x0 AND x1 AND x2), for28 total gates.
Its only refund is the heavy output (gate index9). All81,920 output values,
all92 inputs of weight below3, and the value atY were checked independently;
the existing search.py scalar and bitvector evaluators agree exactly. This
baseline is an upper-bound fixture, not a violating circuit.
