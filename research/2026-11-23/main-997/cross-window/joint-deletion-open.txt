1. Exact open inequality
------------------------
Work in k=|Y|>=3; the checked SmallWindow theorem handles smaller k. Put

 Q_H = sum over targets disjoint Y of max(|S_i|-1,0),
 Q_L = sum over targets meeting Y of (min(k,|S_i|)-1),
 e   = number of empty targets.

Every light target is nonempty. Consequently the exact posted inequality is

 Q_H + Q_L <= N + R + e.                              (J)

After fixing every input in Y to1, h becomes1 and z becomes0. All light
outputs become constant1. The heavy outputs remain their exact linear OR
functions on the outside inputs. The checked heavy OR argument supplies
Q_H distinct heavy OR gate charges. The desired joint deletion accounting is

 N - Q_H + R + e >= Q_L.                              (D)

(D) is an exact reformulation of the remaining count, not an independently
proved deletion theorem. It becomes useful only with a joint construction
that pays the light quotas using removed gates, spare heavy resources, and
whole-set refunds. Constant propagation alone does not supply that argument.
One must include dead cones, aliases and the AND gates bypassed in a heavy
OR extraction. A minimal numerical OR charge selection is not by itself a
proof of a simultaneous circuit rewrite.

2. Full semantic sandwich available at every output
---------------------------------------------------
Let A_w(X)=C1_w(X) and B_w(X)=C0_w(X), where the sole NOT output is replaced
by1 or0 at the same index. For the actual output F_i=OR(S_i), monotonicity
in the replaced source and agreement on the two h-domains give

 h AND F_i <= B_i <= F_i <= A_i <= h OR F_i.

These are full-input function inequalities. In particular A_i is exact on
every h=0 input, not only below weight k, and B_i is exact on every h=1 input.
The all-negative-input frontier consequence is in activation-clauses.txt.

For outside input X define the paired monotone function

 D_w(X) = A_w(X) AND B_w(Y union X).

At each heavy output D_i is exactly OR(S_i). At an AND gate,

 D_(a AND b) = D_a AND D_b.

At an OR gate,

 D_(a OR b) = D_a OR D_b
             OR (A_a(X) AND B_b(Y union X))
             OR (A_b(X) AND B_a(Y union X)).

The two last terms are genuine cross births. Bounding them by one unit of
new gate capacity requires context accounting; neither their singleton
supports nor existing owner pools suffice.

3. Corrected four-type reduction
-------------------------------
Classify a wire by its actual values at the zero input and at Y. An overlap
between a proper C1-private light class and a C0-sound heavy class has type00:
its C1 singleton support is proper, so its value at0 is0; heavy soundness and
Y disjointness make its value atY zero. An OR gate of type00 has both children
of type00. Thus the OR-only predecessor region of each potentially shared
merge consists entirely of type00 wires and terminates at outside raw inputs
or type00 AND entries. Unary/invalid ports may be contracted or treated as
false in this paper description; the source model's exact semantics remain
those of Circuit.

This does NOT classify every retained heavy OR as type00. For example,
(z OR a) OR b has type10 and becomes a OR b after Y=1. Type10 OR gates have
universal C1 singleton support and cannot belong to a proper light private
class. They provide heavy-only capacity. The hard sharing is confined to00
OR regions and their AND/context boundaries. Constants and an exceptional
full-universe target must be handled separately; the difficult branch with
multiple nontrivial heavy targets has proper light targets automatically.

5. Honest stopping point
------------------------
The full semantic sandwich, fixed-frontier activation requirements, and00
component reduction are concrete structural information. None currently
implies a disjoint or amortized bound on all cross births. A proof of(D)
requires a global capacity invariant for context gates shared across several
light/heavy owners, or a gate-elimination argument that establishes the same
count. The existing29 checked special-case helpers and these diagnostics do
not close that lemma. The desk outcome is missing_lemma, not exhausted.
