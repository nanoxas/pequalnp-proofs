Strengthened L-meta-670 research checkpoint
Frozen status: see global14-strengthened-status-snapshot.json.
This checkpoint contains supporting research, not an official proof submission.

The original assigned range concerns at most 15 gates with first gate AND(4,6).
The new search covers a necessary class of global 14-gate witnesses for the same
nine-input function. It constrains all 512 assignments, permits arbitrary NOT
count, and uses a false sentinel matching unavailable-wire semantics.

Four local Lean modules passed with standard axioms only:
  FirstGateReduction: preserves the first gate through cleaning and reduces
    the assigned size 15 case to existence of a global size 14 circuit.
  ReaderShape: gives a least 14-gate witness with output 22; inputs 4,6,7 each
    have one OR reader whose other operand is an earlier gate.
  DegreeThreeShape: inputs 0,1,2,3,5,8 have at most two reader gates; any two
    are both OR. It also proves a unique-successor fact not used by this run.
  RawGateShape: excludes raw-raw AND gates and forces a second reader for
    each endpoint of a raw-raw OR, handling repeated-input aliases separately.

These helpers explicitly assume the exact accepted L-meta-1007 lower bound
for the eight-input deletion graph and L-meta-517 lower bound for theta233.
Their local objects have not been instantiated with those accepted dependency
objects. The conditional theorem bodies and the target expressions were
independently reviewed. Exact source hashes are in the status snapshot.

Reader clauses count gates once even when an input appears on both ports.
The first gate is NOT(0), OR(0,1), OR(0,2), or OR(0,3). All 12 graph automorphisms
and the core-pair orbit sizes 6/6/3 were checked. Compatibility with full greedy
ordering uses a joint construction: choose an input-only gate from the first
nonempty orbit class, relabel it to its representative, then greedily schedule.
Blindly relabeling an arbitrary old first gate would not establish coverage.
The generic liveness, false-operand and double-NOT reductions use a globally
least witness. They impose no monotonicity or bound on the number of NOTs.

The base fixed-CNF controls passed on the actual graph target: a 20-gate
circuit, a one-row mismatch, and a 22-gate NOT(false) extension. The strengthened
controls passed on a deliberate nonminimal OR-all-inputs circuit of 14 gates,
a one-row mismatch, and its 16-gate NOT(false) extension. The latter controls
exercise the new conservative structural clauses; they do not establish a
lower bound for the graph. Generic normalization was checked on 160 seeded
malformed nonconstant circuits, with all 512 rows evaluated for each.
